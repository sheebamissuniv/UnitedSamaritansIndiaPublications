**United Samaritans India Publications** is a dynamic and socially driven publishing house committed to promoting literature, knowledge, culture, and human values across diverse communities. With a vision to empower authors and inspire readers, the publication actively supports works in multiple Indian and international languages, creating a platform for voices that make a positive impact on society.

The organization is widely recognized for publishing and distributing the remarkable literary works of world record author **Dr. Sheeba Lourdhes**, including hundreds of books released across numerous languages. United Samaritans India Publications also publishes the quarterly English magazine **"Times of United Samaritans India,"** highlighting achievements, social initiatives, education, culture, and humanitarian contributions.

Dedicated to excellence in publishing, printing, and global literary outreach, the organization continues to bridge cultures, celebrate creativity, and nurture meaningful knowledge sharing. Through its publications, it strives to inspire generations, preserve values, and contribute to the advancement of humanity through the power of words.
