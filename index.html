Celebrity Founder Profile

Dr. Sheeba Lourdhes

Dr. Sheeba Lourdhes is the Founder and Editor of United Samaritans India Publications, a celebrated author-writer, international cognitive psychologist, personality development trainer, technocrat, motivational speaker, and social reformer.

Her public profile reflects a rare blend of literary brilliance and social purpose: Chairman of United Samaritans India, Editor and Writer at United Samaritans India Publications, Editor in Chief of Times Of United Samaritans India Magazine, patron of the Indian Red Cross Society, regular blood donor, and a leader devoted to cancer-patient aid and community upliftment.

Record-Breaking Excellence

Internationally recognized publishing achievement for literature with humanitarian purpose.

Asia Book of Records and World Records Union recognized Dr. Sheeba Lourdhes of Tamil Nadu, India, for publishing 250 books in 20 languages to provide aid to cancer patients. Asia Book of Records confirmed the multilingual humanitarian record on March 1, 2025, while World Records Union recognized the achievement as the most books published and released by a single author in the least time, 3 months, officially confirmed on March 20, 2025.

Humanitarian Publishing

Every publication carries the humanitarian heartbeat of United Samaritans India Foundation.

United Samaritans India Publications functions as the literary and knowledge wing of a larger humanitarian ecosystem. The foundation’s work focuses on women, children, differently-abled people, underprivileged communities, animal welfare, education, health care, mental-health support, and life-guidance programs. The publication house gives this mission a global voice through books, magazines, catalogues, awareness writing, and multilingual outreach.

Children, Education And Cancer Care

Every book becomes more than a literary product: it becomes a channel for awareness, learning, dignity, and support for children, including children battling cancer and children who need educational opportunity.

Women Empowerment

The foundation’s women-focused mission is reflected in publications that encourage self-worth, confidence, mental resilience, independence, and leadership.

Mental Health And Life Guidance

The foundation highlights emotional well-being, counselling, psychology, and lifestyle guidance. The publication house amplifies these values through motivational, psychological, and self-help writing.

Underprivileged Communities

United Samaritans India Foundation works to support vulnerable lives with dignity-oriented care, including education, food, shelter, clothing, medication, and pathways to livelihood.

Differently-Abled Support

The foundation’s service vision includes differently-abled individuals, rehabilitation support, social inclusion, and opportunities to participate meaningfully in society.

Animal Welfare And Compassion

The foundation also extends compassion to abandoned and abused animals, making the publication mission part of a wider culture of rescue, protection, and recovery.
